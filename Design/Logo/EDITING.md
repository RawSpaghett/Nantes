# Nantes logo

These are the current logo files:

- `Nantes-Logo-Original.svg`: six editable vector letters.
- `Nantes-Logo-Original.psd`: six letter layers on a transparent background.
- `Nantes-Logo-Original.png`: transparent export.

Use the SVG for shape changes or the PSD for pixel edits. Export to `Assets/NantesUI/Logo/NantesLogo.png` for Unity. Keep the background transparent.

The font is Nantes Display, a modified Michroma with an open A. Its license is in `Assets/NantesUI/Fonts/OFL.txt`.

The glitch and ship overlap are Unity effects; they are not part of the artwork.
